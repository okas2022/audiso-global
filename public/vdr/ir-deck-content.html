AudisoStrategy · Competitive Gap

The Audiso Advantage — Closing Audiology's Digital Gap

Why incumbent DX still stops at hearing-aid hardware sales — and where Audiso sits instead.

The structural pain point

Why has global hearing DX remained stuck at device sell-through? Tier-1 OEMs are optimized for fitting and shipping hardware. A staff-free, preventive screening system that resolves demand before the clinic visit is economically awkward for a P&L that grows with device units. Prevention that keeps patients out of the store looks like self-cannibalization — so product roadmaps stay hardware-first, with apps bolted on as accessories.

Incumbents are built to sell hearing aids. Audiso is built to own the diagnostic data layer that decides who needs care — and when — before the aid is ever opened.
