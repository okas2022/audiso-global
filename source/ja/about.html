Clinical leadership

Young-joon Seo, M.D., Ph.D. — Professor of Otolaryngology, Yonsei University Wonju College of Medicine · CEO, Audiso Co., Ltd.

Audiso is not a consumer gadget brand. It is a clinically grounded hearing-health company built on two decades of university-hospital research in hearing loss, tinnitus, and cognitive protection — presented to global audiologists at WCA 2026 Seoul.

Korea's only KOLAS-accredited audiometer calibration laboratory (ISO/IEC 17025) underpins every algorithm in our ecosystem — from kiosk screening to home digital therapeutics.

私たちの事業

延世大学原州セブランス基督教病院ENT専門医のもと、Audisoは韓国唯一のKOLAS認証聴力計校正ラボを運営し、病院・公共機関・企業に精密聴力ケアを提供します。
